Frequently asked questions

What is the Mifflin-St Jeor equation?

It is the formula most dietitians consider the most accurate simple estimate of resting calorie burn: for men, BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + 5; for women the same formula ends with − 161 instead of + 5.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the energy your body uses at complete rest just to stay alive. TDEE (maintenance calories) multiplies BMR by an activity factor to include movement and exercise — that is the level at which your weight stays roughly the same.

How fast will I lose weight at −500 calories a day?

A 500 kcal daily deficit is roughly 0.45 kg (about 1 lb) per week in theory, because a kilogram of body fat stores around 7,700 kcal. Real results vary with water weight, muscle mass and how activity changes over time.

How accurate is this calculator?

For most people the estimate is within roughly ±10% of measured values. It cannot account for body composition, medical conditions, medication or pregnancy, which is why it is an estimate for general information — not medical advice.
