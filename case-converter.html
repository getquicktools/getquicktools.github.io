Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalises the first letter of every word ("The Quick Brown Fox"), which suits headlines. Sentence case capitalises only the first letter of each sentence ("The quick brown fox"), which suits normal writing.

What are camelCase and snake_case used for?

They are naming styles for code and file names. camelCase joins words with each new word capitalised (myVariableName); snake_case joins them with underscores (my_variable_name).

I typed in ALL CAPS by accident — can this fix it?

Yes. Paste the text and click Sentence case or lowercase to turn shouting caps into normal, readable text.

Does converting change my word count?

Only camelCase and snake_case join words together, which changes how words are separated. The character count shown always reflects the current text exactly.
