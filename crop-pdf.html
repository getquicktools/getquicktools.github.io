Crop PDF

Trim unwanted margins and white space from every page of a PDF. Enter how many points to cut from each side — 72 points equal one inch (25.4 mm).

🔒 Private by design: your PDF is cropped locally in your browser — it is never uploaded to any server.

How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the box, or click it to select a file. The current page size is shown.
  2. Type how much to trim from the top, bottom, left and right, in points (72 pt = 1 inch ≈ 25.4 mm). Leave a side at 0 to keep it.
  3. Click Crop & download. The same crop is applied to every page.

Frequently asked questions

What does cropping a PDF actually do?

It shrinks the visible page area (the crop box) by the amounts you enter, which trims away margins and white space so the content appears larger when viewed or printed. This is the standard way to remove the wide white borders that scans and exported documents often have.

Is the cropped-away content deleted?

Cropping changes which part of the page is shown; the underlying content outside the crop area usually still exists in the file and can be revealed by resetting the crop in a full PDF editor. Don't rely on cropping to hide sensitive information — use a redaction tool for that.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Cropping is done entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

Can I crop a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser library. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then crop.
