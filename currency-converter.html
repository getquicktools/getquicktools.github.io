Frequently asked questions

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Rates are fetched live from the Open Exchange Rates API (open.er-api.com), which publishes mid-market rates collected from financial data providers. The "Rates updated" line under the result shows when the table was last refreshed.

Why is the rate different at my bank or money changer?

Banks and exchange shops add a margin (spread) and sometimes fees on top of the mid-market rate, so you will usually receive slightly less than the figure shown here. This converter is best for checking the fair, real-world value of a currency.

Does the converter work offline?

No — it needs an internet connection once to download the latest rate table. If the rates fail to load, an error is shown and conversion is disabled rather than guessing with stale numbers.

Are the amounts I enter sent anywhere?

No. Your browser downloads the public rate table, and the maths happens on your device. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored.
