How to use

  1. For a duration, pick a start and end date in the first card — you get total days, weeks, and a years/months/days breakdown.
  2. For a deadline or future date, use the second card: pick a date, enter the number of days, and choose add or subtract.
  3. Results update instantly as you change any value.

Frequently asked questions

Does the count include the start and end dates?

The total is the number of full days between the two dates — the standard difference. From Monday to Tuesday is 1 day. If you need an inclusive count (both endpoints counted, e.g. for hotel nights vs days), simply add 1.

How is the years/months/days breakdown worked out?

It is a calendar breakdown, the same way you would describe an age: whole years first, then whole months, then the remaining days. Because months have different lengths, it is not just the total divided by 30.

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. All maths uses the real calendar, so February 29 in leap years is counted correctly in both directions.

Which day of the week was a date?

Use the "add or subtract days" card with 0 days — the result shows the full date including its weekday. The "days between" result also names both weekdays.
