Good to know

Exact age is more than subtracting years: if this year's birthday has not happened yet, you are a year younger than the simple subtraction says. Forms, schools, sports categories and official records all care about the exact figure, down to months and days.

  • Official cut-offs (school admission, sports age groups) usually count age on a specific date — calculate age on that date, not today.
  • Your date of birth on your ID card is the one that counts for any official purpose, even if your family celebrates a different day.
  • Age in days is a fun check: most people are surprised they are only around ten thousand days old at 27.

Frequently asked questions

How is the exact age calculated?

We subtract the birth date from today, borrowing days from the previous month and months from the previous year where needed — the same method used on official forms, so "27 years, 4 months, 12 days" is precise.

Does total days lived include today?

It counts the full days between the birth date and today, not counting the partial current day — the standard convention for age calculators.

Can I calculate the age of something other than a person?

Yes — it works for any start date: a business, a pet, a project or an anniversary. Just enter the date it began.

What if I enter a future date?

The calculator will warn you — a date of birth must be in the past for an age to make sense.
