How to Make a PDF File Smaller

A PDF that is too big to email or upload is a common headache — and almost always fixable in a minute. Here are the three methods that actually work, and when to use each.

Why is my PDF so big?

Almost every oversized PDF has the same cause: images. A single scanned page saved at high resolution can weigh several megabytes. Long text documents, on the other hand, are usually tiny — text itself takes almost no space. So the right way to shrink a PDF depends on what is inside it.

Method 1: compress it online (best for scanned PDFs)

The free QuickTools Compress PDF tool redraws each page at a lower, screen-friendly resolution. For scanned documents and image-heavy PDFs this typically removes 70–95% of the file size, and it happens entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

  1. Open the Compress PDF tool.
  2. Drop in your PDF and choose a quality level — start with the middle setting.
  3. Download the compressed PDF and compare: the tool shows the old and new sizes.

One honest trade-off: this method turns each page into an image, so text in the compressed file is no longer selectable or searchable. For scanned documents that changes nothing (their text was already a picture), but for a text-heavy report, try Method 2 first.

Method 2: re-save at lower quality (keeps text selectable)

Most desktop PDF viewers can shrink a file while keeping its text intact. Open the PDF, choose Print, select a "Microsoft Print to PDF" / "Save as PDF" printer, and in the advanced settings pick a lower output quality. Quality tools such as Adobe Acrobat's "Reduce File Size" do the same job with one click. Results vary — expect 20–60% savings on mixed documents.

Method 3: remove pages you do not need

Sometimes the simplest compression is deleting bulk. If a 60-page report is large because of an appendix nobody reads, split it with the Split PDF tool and keep only the pages that matter, or tidy it with the Organize PDF tool. Sending several smaller files that add up to less is also an option — combine the keepers afterwards with Merge PDF.

How small should you aim for?

  • Email: Gmail and most providers cap attachments at 25 MB — aim for under 10 MB to be safe with other attachments.
  • Government and job portals: upload limits are often 2–5 MB per document.
  • WhatsApp: documents up to 100 MB send fine, but smaller files download faster for the receiver.

Frequently asked questions

Will compression ruin the quality?

At the middle quality setting, pages stay perfectly readable on screen and in normal print. Only extreme zoom reveals softness — that is the trade that removes most of the weight.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs locally in your browser, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

My PDF barely shrank. Why?

If the file is mostly text, there is little image data to remove — it was already small in the ways that matter. Removing unneeded pages (Method 3) helps more in that case.

What is the maximum file size?

There is no fixed limit; very large files are only limited by your device's memory.
