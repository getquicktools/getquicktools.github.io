How to Convert JPG Images to a PDF

Turn one or more images into a single PDF document. Arrange the images in order, choose the page size, orientation and margins, then download.

Step by step: with the free JPG to PDF tool

The free QuickTools JPG to PDF runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, and your files and data are never uploaded to a server.

  1. Drop your images into the box, or click it to select several at once.
  2. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to arrange them — each image becomes one page, in the order shown.
  3. Choose page size, orientation and margin. "Fit to image" makes every page exactly the size of its image.

Good to know

Many offices, schools and government portals ask for documents 'as a PDF', even when all you have is photos of the pages. Converting your JPGs into one PDF gives you a single tidy file that prints predictably and is accepted everywhere.

  • Put the images in reading order before converting — the PDF follows the list order exactly.
  • Use the highest-quality photos you have; the PDF keeps the image quality you feed it.
  • One PDF per application or submission keeps files small and easy to name.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is built entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library, so private photos and scans stay on your device.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG and PNG are embedded directly. WebP (and other formats your browser can display) are converted to PNG in the browser first, then added.

Will my images be compressed?

JPG images are embedded unchanged, so their quality is preserved exactly. PNG images are also embedded losslessly.

How are images placed on the page?

Each image is scaled to fit inside the page margins while keeping its proportions, and centred on the page. It is never stretched or cropped.
