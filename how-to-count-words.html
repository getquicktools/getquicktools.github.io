Good to know

Word limits are everywhere: 500-word essays, 250-word personal statements, character caps on posts and forms. Counting by hand is hopeless — paste the text into a counter and you get words, characters, sentences and reading time at once.

  • Count early, not at the deadline — trimming 10% from a finished essay is harder than writing to length.
  • Character limits include spaces unless stated otherwise; check both numbers.
  • Reading time assumes roughly 200 words per minute — a 1,000-word article is about a 5-minute read.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes an average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute. A 1,000-word article therefore takes roughly 5 minutes to read.

What counts as a word?

Any sequence of characters separated by spaces, tabs or line breaks counts as one word — so "hello-world" counts as one word, and numbers count as words too.

How are sentences counted?

Sentences are counted by ending punctuation (. ! ?). Text without ending punctuation counts as a single sentence while you are still writing it.

Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything is counted in your browser the moment you type. Close the tab and the text is gone.
