How to Add Text and Images to a PDF

Add new text or an image on top of any page of a PDF: a note, a date, your name, a logo. Click on the page preview to choose exactly where the new content will go, then download the result.

Step by step: with the free Edit PDF tool

The free QuickTools Edit PDF runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, and your files and data are never uploaded to a server.

  1. Drop your PDF into the box, or click it to select a file. The first page is shown as a preview.
  2. Use the page controls to open the page you want to change, then click on the preview where the new text or image should start (a red dot marks the spot).
  3. Type your text, set its size and colour, and click Add text at clicked point — or choose an image and click Add image at clicked point.
  4. Repeat on any pages you like, then click Apply & download edited PDF.

Good to know

Not every PDF edit needs the original document or expensive software. Adding a name and date to a form, a note in the margin, or your logo on a quotation can be done by placing new content on top of the existing pages.

  • This kind of editing adds content on top of pages — it does not rewrite the PDF's original text.
  • Match the font size and colour to the document so additions look native.
  • Place images at the size you need before adding — resizing afterwards is limited.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change or delete text that is already in the PDF?

No — honestly, this tool only adds new content on top of pages. Changing or removing existing text requires a full PDF editor. A common workaround is to add your correction as new text, but the original words remain underneath.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The preview is rendered and the new content is drawn entirely in your browser using the pdf.js and pdf-lib libraries, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

How accurate is the placement?

The clicked point becomes the top-left corner of the new text or image. The preview is a scaled render of the page, and the position is converted back to exact PDF coordinates, so the result lands where you clicked.

Can I edit a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser libraries. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then edit.
