How to Merge PDF Files Online for Free

Combining several PDFs into one clean document takes less than a minute — here is the fastest way to do it, plus the tips that keep the result tidy.

The fastest way: merge PDFs right in your browser

You do not need to install software or create an account. The free QuickTools Merge PDF tool combines your files locally in your browser — the PDFs are never uploaded to a server, which makes it safe even for contracts, ID documents and bank statements.

  1. Open the Merge PDF tool.
  2. Drop your PDF files into the box, or click the box to select several files at once.
  3. Arrange the files with the ↑ and ↓ buttons — the merged document follows exactly this order.
  4. Click Merge & download merged.pdf. Your combined PDF downloads immediately.

Tips for a clean merged PDF

  • Order matters. Put the cover page or main document first, and annexes or supporting documents after it.
  • Only need some pages? Split the file first with the Split PDF tool, then merge just the parts you need.
  • Pages in the wrong rotation or order? Fix them afterwards with the Organize PDF tool — you can reorder, rotate and delete pages in one place.
  • Result too large to email? Read our guide on how to make a PDF file smaller.
  • Remove duplicates before merging. Merging the same file twice doubles those pages — check the file list before you click Merge.

Other ways to combine PDF files

Desktop software such as Adobe Acrobat can merge PDFs, but it is paid software and overkill for a one-off task. Print to PDF — opening each file and printing it into a single virtual printer — works in a pinch but often loses bookmarks, links and image quality. For most people, a browser tool is the fastest option: nothing to install, and on QuickTools your files stay on your own device instead of being uploaded to a stranger's server.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The Merge PDF tool is free with no sign-up, no page limit and no watermark on the result.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The merging happens entirely in your browser using a JavaScript PDF library. Your files never leave your device, so the tool is safe for private documents.

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

There is no fixed limit. Dozens of typical documents merge in seconds; the only limit is your device's memory.

Can I change the page order after merging?

Yes — either arrange the files before merging, or rearrange individual pages afterwards with the Organize PDF tool.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted PDFs cannot be read by the browser. Remove the password in your usual PDF viewer first, then merge.
