How to Remove the Background from an Image for Free

Transparent backgrounds used to need expensive software and a steady hand. Now an AI in your browser does it in seconds — here is how, and how to get clean, professional edges.

Remove a background in seconds — with AI, in your browser

The free QuickTools Background Remover uses an AI model that runs entirely in your browser. Unlike most background removers, your photo is never uploaded — the AI works on your own device, so even personal photos stay private.

  1. Open the Background Remover tool.
  2. Choose or drop your image (JPG or PNG works best).
  3. Wait a few seconds while the AI separates the subject from the background. On your very first use, the AI model downloads once — after that it is instant.
  4. Download your image as a transparent PNG.

Tips for clean edges

  • Contrast helps. A subject that stands out clearly from its background — different colours or brightness — gives the cleanest cut-out.
  • Start from the original. Use the highest-resolution photo you have; you can always shrink it later with the Resize Image tool.
  • Hair and fur are the hardest edges for any AI. A plainer background behind the subject makes a visible difference.
  • Product photos: shoot the item on a plain, evenly lit surface and the result looks studio-made — perfect for marketplace listings.

What to do with a transparent image

A transparent PNG can be placed on any colour or design: product listings on white, logos on websites, stickers, thumbnails and presentations. Need it smaller for a listing or a website? Run it through the Image Compressor. Need a different format instead? The Image Converter switches between PNG, JPG and WebP — note that JPG does not support transparency, so keep transparent images as PNG or WebP.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many images you process.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The AI model runs in your browser on your device. The only download is the model itself, the first time you use the tool.

Why did the first image take longer?

The AI model (a few tens of MB) downloads once and is then cached by your browser. Later images process in seconds.

Which format should I download?

PNG keeps the transparent background. If you save as JPG, the transparent area becomes white, because JPG cannot store transparency.
