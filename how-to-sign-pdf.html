How to Sign a PDF Online

Add your signature to a PDF. Draw it with your mouse or finger, or type your name and have it rendered in a handwriting style — then stamp it on the page you choose.

Step by step: with the free Sign PDF tool

The free QuickTools Sign PDF runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, and your files and data are never uploaded to a server.

  1. Drop the PDF you want to sign into the box, or click it to select a file.
  2. Choose Draw it and sign in the box with your mouse or finger — or choose Type my name for a script-style signature.
  3. Pick which page to sign (the last page is selected by default), where it goes, and how wide it should be.
  4. Click Sign & download.

Good to know

Printing a form just to sign it and scan it back is a chore from another era. Signing in the browser lets you draw your signature with a mouse or finger and place it on the page — the document never leaves your device, which matters for personal paperwork.

  • Sign on a copy and keep the unsigned original — you may need a clean version later.
  • Draw your signature a few times and keep the attempt that looks most like your real one.
  • For legally binding contracts, check what your country or counterparty requires — some need certified digital signatures rather than a drawn one.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a legally binding digital signature?

This tool places a visible image of your signature on the page — an "electronic signature" in appearance. It is not a cryptographic digital signature with a certificate, which some legal, banking or government processes specifically require.

Is my PDF or signature uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library. Neither the document nor your signature image ever leaves your device.

The typed signature uses which font?

Your name is rendered on a canvas using your device's handwriting-style fonts (such as Brush Script or Segoe Script). If none are installed, a generic cursive font is used, so results vary slightly between devices.

Can I sign a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser library. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then sign.
