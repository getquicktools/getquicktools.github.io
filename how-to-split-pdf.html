How to Split a PDF and Extract Pages

Pull pages out of a PDF. Either extract one page range into a single new PDF, or split every page into its own PDF file and download them all as a ZIP.

Step by step: with the free Split PDF tool

The free QuickTools Split PDF runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, and your files and data are never uploaded to a server.

  1. Drop your PDF into the box, or click it to select a file. The page count is shown.
  2. To keep only some pages: type the first and last page of the range and click Extract range as one PDF.
  3. To turn every page into a separate file: click Split all pages and a ZIP with one PDF per page will download.

Good to know

Splitting is what you do when a PDF contains more than you want to send: one chapter of a report, a single invoice from a monthly statement, or the signed pages of a contract. Instead of forwarding a 60-page file, you extract exactly the pages that matter.

  • Decide the exact page numbers before you start — open the PDF and note the range, so you only extract once.
  • To keep several separate sections, split once per section and recombine them with Merge PDF.
  • Extracted pages keep their original quality — splitting copies pages exactly, it does not re-compress them.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Splitting is done entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

Will the extracted pages lose quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are — text, images and resolution are preserved. Nothing is re-compressed.

What is inside the ZIP file?

One PDF per page, named page-1.pdf, page-2.pdf and so on, in the original page order.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser library. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then split.
