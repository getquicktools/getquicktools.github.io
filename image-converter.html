Frequently asked questions

Which format should I choose?

Choose JPG for photos and maximum compatibility, PNG when you need transparency or perfectly sharp edges (logos, screenshots), and WebP for websites where small file size matters most.

What happens to transparency when I convert to JPG?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are placed on a white background. Convert to PNG or WebP to keep transparency intact.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no upload limit because nothing is uploaded — the practical limit is your device's memory. Very large images (e.g. 100MP) may be slow on older phones.

Are my images stored anywhere?

No. The conversion is done by your browser alone, and the file disappears as soon as you close the tab.
