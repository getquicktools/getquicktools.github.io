Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on QuickTools is completely free to use, with no limits, no watermarks and no premium tier. Use them as often as you like, for personal or commercial projects.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Your images, PDFs and text never leave your device, which makes QuickTools safe for private documents and personal photos.

Do I need an account?

No account, no sign-up and no email address. Just open a tool and use it — there is nothing to install and nothing to log into.

Do the tools work on phones?

Yes. QuickTools is fully responsive and works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. A few heavy tools — the background remover and video compressor run AI and video engines on your device — work best on a desktop.
