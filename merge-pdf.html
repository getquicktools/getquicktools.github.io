Merge PDF

Combine several PDF files into a single document. Add your files, put them in the order you want, and download the merged PDF.

🔒 Private by design: your PDFs are merged locally in your browser — they are never uploaded to any server.

    How to use

    1. Drop your PDF files into the box, or click it to select several files at once.
    2. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to arrange the files — pages are merged in the order shown.
    3. Click Merge & download merged.pdf.
    4. Your combined file downloads as merged.pdf.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

    No. Merging is done entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    There is no fixed limit. Very large sets of files are only limited by your device's memory — dozens of typical documents merge in seconds.

    Will the quality of my PDFs change?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are — text, images, links and resolution are preserved. Nothing is re-compressed.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser library. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then merge.
