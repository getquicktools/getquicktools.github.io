How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the box, or click it to select a file. Thumbnails of all pages appear.
  2. Use the ← and → buttons under a page to move it earlier or later. Use ✕ to delete a page.
  3. The bold number under each page shows its position in the new document.
  4. Click Save in this order & download to get the reorganized PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Thumbnails are rendered and the new PDF is built entirely in your browser, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

Will the saved pages lose quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are into the new file — text, images and resolution are preserved.

I deleted a page by mistake. Can I get it back?

Click "Reset order" to restore all pages in their original order, as long as you haven't closed the page. Your original file on disk is never changed.

Can I organize a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser libraries. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then organize.
