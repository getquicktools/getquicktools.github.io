How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the box, or click it to select a file.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Pages are rendered entirely in your browser using the pdf.js library, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

What resolution are the JPGs?

Pages are rendered at 2× their PDF size, which is sharp enough for documents, slides and social sharing. A standard A4 page comes out at roughly 1654 × 2339 pixels.

Can I convert a very long PDF?

Yes, but each page is rendered in your browser, so a PDF with hundreds of pages can take a while and use a lot of memory. For very long documents, split the PDF first and convert it in parts.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser library. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then convert.
