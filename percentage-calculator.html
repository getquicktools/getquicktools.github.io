Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30. The first calculator does this for you.

How is percentage increase calculated?

Take the difference between the new and old values, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. Going from 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = a 25% increase.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

If interest rises from 4% to 5%, that is a 1 percentage point rise — but a 25% increase in the rate itself. The two ideas are often confused in news reports.

Can a percentage be more than 100%?

Yes. If something doubles, it has increased by 100% and is now 200% of its original value. Proportions can also exceed 100% whenever the part is bigger than the whole you compare it with.
