How to use

  1. Drop a JPG, PNG or WebP image into the box, or click to browse.
  2. Click Remove background. The first time, the AI model downloads (tens of MB) — this happens only once.
  3. Wait for processing to finish; watch the progress bar.
  4. Check the transparent preview and click Download PNG.

Frequently asked questions

How does the background removal work?

An AI segmentation model (the open-source @imgly/background-removal library) runs entirely in your browser, detects the main subject, and produces a PNG with a transparent background. Your image is never sent to our servers — there are none.

Why is the first use slow?

The AI model files (tens of MB) must be downloaded from the library's CDN the first time, and the model itself needs a few seconds to run — especially on phones. Later uses in the same visit are faster because the model stays loaded.

What kinds of photos work best?

Photos with one clear subject and a distinct outline — people, products, pets, cars — work best. Hair, glass, smoke and subjects that blend into a similar-coloured background can leave rough edges, as with any automatic tool.

Why is the download always a PNG?

Transparency requires a format with an alpha channel. PNG is the universal choice; JPG cannot store transparency at all, so removed backgrounds are always delivered as PNG.
