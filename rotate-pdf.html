Rotate PDF

Fix sideways or upside-down pages. Rotate every page in a PDF — or just a page range — by 90, 180 or 270 degrees and download the corrected file.

🔒 Private by design: your PDF is rotated locally in your browser — it is never uploaded to any server.

How to use

  1. Drop your PDF into the box, or click it to select a file. The page count is shown.
  2. Pick the rotation angle — 90°, 180° or 270° clockwise.
  3. Choose whether to rotate all pages, or type a page range to rotate only those pages.
  4. Click Rotate & download and save the corrected PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Rotation is done entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

Does rotating change the content or quality?

No. Only the page orientation flag changes — text, images and resolution are untouched, and the rotation is added to any rotation the pages already have.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Choose "Page range only" and set both the From and To values to that page's number.

Can I rotate a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser library. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then rotate.
