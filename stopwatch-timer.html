    How to use

    1. Stopwatch: press Start, use Lap to record split times, and Reset to clear.
    2. Countdown: set the minutes and seconds, press Start, and watch the progress bar fill.
    3. Pause either timer at any point and resume where you left off.

    Frequently asked questions

    How accurate is the stopwatch?

    It is timed from your device's high-resolution clock and displayed to hundredths of a second, so it is as accurate as a typical phone stopwatch. The display refreshes about 30 times per second.

    Will the countdown beep if I switch to another tab?

    The timer keeps counting in the background, but browsers may delay the beep slightly in an inactive tab, and a muted device will not make a sound. For important alarms, keep the tab visible and the volume up.

    What is a lap time?

    Each lap records the total elapsed time at the moment you press Lap, plus the time since the previous lap (shown in brackets) — the standard way to track intervals in running, swimming or cooking steps.

    Is there a maximum time?

    The stopwatch counts for as long as the tab stays open (minutes simply keep growing past 59). The countdown accepts up to 999 minutes plus 59 seconds.
