Text to Speech

Type or paste any text and hear it read aloud. Choose a voice, change the speed and pitch, and pause or resume whenever you like.

🔒 Private by design: speech is generated by your own browser — nothing is uploaded or stored. (Note: some browsers use online voices provided by the browser maker; the available voices depend on your browser and device.)

Voices are supplied by your browser and device, so the list differs between phones and computers. No audio file is produced — playback only.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Pick a voice from the list — voices marked with a language you know will pronounce your text best.
  3. Adjust the speed (0.5×–2×) and pitch sliders to taste.
  4. Press Speak. Use Pause, Resume and Stop to control playback.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I see different voices on my phone and my computer?

The voices come from your browser and operating system, not from this website. Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox each bundle or borrow different voices, and phones offer their own system voices — so the list is never the same on two devices.

Can I download the speech as an MP3?

No. The browser's speech feature is playback-only and does not provide an audio file. If you need a recording, play the speech aloud and capture it with a screen or audio recorder.

Which voice should I choose for another language?

Choose a voice whose language code matches your text (for example ur-PK for Urdu, hi-IN for Hindi, es-ES for Spanish). A mismatched voice will still speak, but pronunciation may sound odd.

Why did nothing happen when I pressed Speak?

Some browsers only load voices after a moment, and a few require the page to have sound permission. Wait a second and try again, check your volume, and make sure the text box is not empty. On iPhones, also turn off the silent switch.
