How to use

  1. Type the bill amount.
  2. Tap a preset tip percentage, or enter your own in the custom box.
  3. Set how many people are splitting the bill.
  4. Read the tip, the total, and each person's share.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

It varies by country and service. In the US, 15–20% is standard for table service at restaurants. In much of Europe a smaller tip or rounding up is common, and in Japan tipping is generally not expected. When in doubt, 15% is a safe middle ground.

Is the tip calculated before or after tax?

Traditionally the tip is based on the pre-tax subtotal. This calculator applies the percentage to the amount you enter, so if you want a pre-tax tip, enter the subtotal from your bill rather than the grand total.

How does splitting work?

The total bill plus tip is divided equally by the number of people, and the tip itself is also shown per person so everyone can see their share of both parts.

What if we're not splitting evenly?

Run the calculator once per person with their own subtotal as the "bill amount" and people set to 1 — each run gives that person's exact tip and total.
