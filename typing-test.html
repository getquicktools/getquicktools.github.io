How to use

  1. Click the typing box and start typing the paragraph exactly as shown — the timer starts on your first character.
  2. Correct letters turn green; mistakes turn red. The live WPM and accuracy update as you go.

  4. Press Restart to try again with a different text.

Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

By the standard convention of 5 characters = 1 word. Gross WPM counts every character you typed; net WPM counts only correct characters, so it rewards accuracy as well as speed. Both are divided by the time taken, in minutes.

What is a good typing speed?

The average person types around 40 WPM. 55–70 WPM is fast and typical of experienced office workers, and professional typists often exceed 80–100 WPM. Accuracy above 95% matters more than raw speed for real work.

Can I paste text into the box?

No — pasting is disabled, because that would measure your clipboard, not your typing. Backspace works normally if you want to correct a mistake.

Why did the test end before 60 seconds?

If you type the whole paragraph correctly to the end, the test finishes early and your scores are calculated from the actual time you used.
