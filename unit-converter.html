Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and add 32. So 20°C × 9/5 + 32 = 68°F. The temperature category handles Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin for you.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram equals about 2.20462 pounds, and one pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms by international definition.

Is a ton here a metric tonne?

Yes — the weight list uses the metric ton (tonne) of 1,000 kg, the standard in most of the world. Note that a US "short ton" is 2,000 lb (≈ 907 kg), which is different.

How accurate are the results?

Conversions use exact international definitions (e.g. 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly) and results are shown to up to 10 significant figures, trimmed of trailing zeros.
