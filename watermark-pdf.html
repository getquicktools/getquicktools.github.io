Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The watermark is stamped entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library, so even confidential documents stay on your device.

Is the watermark permanent?

The text is drawn onto each page as part of the PDF content. It cannot be removed with a simple setting, but like any watermark it should be treated as a visible deterrent, not as encryption or security.

Which colour is the watermark?

Grey, at the opacity you choose. Grey stays visible on both light and dark page content without hiding the text underneath.

Can I watermark a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be read by the browser library. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then add the watermark.
